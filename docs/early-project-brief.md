# Early Project Brief

## Problem
Investors cannot compare stocks responsibly by looking at historical returns alone. Risk exposure and relationships between assets affect both stock-level evaluation and portfolio outcomes.

## Proposed approach
Use CAPM to estimate systematic market exposure and model-implied return. Use MPT to estimate portfolio risk from expected returns and covariance, then compare minimum-variance and maximum-Sharpe allocations with an equal-weighted baseline.

## Proposed universe
A multi-sector sample of ten stocks across five sectors, subject to a data-feasibility audit. The market/exchange is not yet frozen.

## Expected evidence
The project should deliver reproducible descriptive statistics, CAPM regression results, correlation/covariance analysis, portfolio allocations, an efficient-frontier plot and an out-of-sample comparison.

## Limitations to acknowledge
Historical estimates are uncertain; portfolio optimization may amplify estimation error; a fixed stock sample can create selection bias; and historical performance does not guarantee future outcomes.
